# Code of Conduct

MeetAgain is built for people who want to meet each other. Let's treat each other that way here too.

## Be nice

- Be friendly and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any form.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Don't share anyone's private information.

That's it. Common sense covers the rest.

## If something goes wrong

If someone makes you feel unwelcome, contact the maintainer at xuedi@meetagain.org. Reports are kept confidential.
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or block people who keep ignoring this.

This applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any other project space.
